# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Product targeting per suggestion mode (#153): how a product's target inside its category is held (equal share, a weight, or keep current) is now set per product and per mode (fixed add, buy-only-to-target, full rebalance, fixed sell) in the product list of the Rebalance tab, in the "Share in the category" column for the mode chosen in the form. Each mode keeps its own setting and a suggestion uses the setting of the mode it runs; modes are not kept in step (#152). A product without a setting is an equal share. The settings are global (the same in every account); an account with its own strategy keeps its own ratios. The balance table of an account uses the fixed-add setting. New `Portfolio::product_shares` (edit `SetProductShare`), `product_targets.csv` in the export.
- Trading participation moves into the Rebalance tab (#151): every account tab and each account on All show the same product list, with a "Show all" / "Tradable only" toggle, a "takes part in trading" checkbox per product (held or not), select all / clear, and a note under the table. The account's "sell allowed" flag and rounding unit are edited there too. Target and Short(+)/over(-) are no longer blank: Free categories show an equal share, Keep-current categories the current value.
- Per-account strategies (#143): an account follows the portfolio (default) or aims at specified products with hand-typed ratios (blank = equal share). `Account::strategy` / `Strategy::Products` (edit `SetStrategy`, the "Strategy" column of the Accounts card, `accounts.csv` columns `strategy` and `strategy_products`). In the account's Rebalance tab the balance table shows the listed products against their ratios of their total and the four suggestion modes spread money over them with the existing water-filling and rounding (category targets are not used, products outside the list and excluded ones are never traded). The All tab leaves such an account out. Schema version 18 (an older app refuses the file); an older file follows the portfolio.
- The Rebalance "All" tab now trades across every account (#148): it lists, per account, the products it holds with checkboxes (the per-account exclusion list of #142, ticked = not excluded) and "Select all" / "Clear" buttons. The suggestion engine (`suggest::Request.account` is now optional, `ProductLine` carries its account) spreads a product's amount over the accounts that may buy or sell it and rounds per account (the total to the least common multiple of the rounding units). Results are grouped by account.
- Per-account exclusion of products from trading (#142): `Account::excluded` (edit `SetExcluded`, "Left out of trading" in the Accounts card of the settings tab) lists products that suggestions never buy or sell in that account, in any mode; their holdings still count toward the category's value. The Rebalance account tab marks them "Excluded". `accounts.csv` gets `excluded_product_ids`. Schema version 16 (an older app refuses the file); an older file loads with nothing excluded, no conversion needed.

### Changed

- Schema version 20 replaces `Category::targeting` and `Holding::weight` with `Portfolio::product_shares`. An older file is converted on load: a weighted category gives each product the sum of its record weights (an equal share when it had none), a keep-current category keeps the current values, and the same setting is put into all four modes; free and equal categories store nothing (equal is the default). An older app refuses the new file. The Categories card of Settings loses its "Product targeting" column, the Records card loses the weight inputs, `holdings.csv` loses `relative_weight`, `categories.csv` loses `product_targeting`, and the "weighted product without a weight" and "category has no products" problems are gone (the edit `SetHoldingWeight` and the `targeting` field of `UpdateCategory` too).
- Schema version 19 merges the buyable products (`Account::tradable`, edit `SetTradable`) into the exclusion list (`Account::excluded`): a product takes part in trading unless it is excluded. An older file is converted when read (excluded = old excluded plus every product outside a set `tradable`); `accounts.csv` loses `tradable_product_ids`. Settings > Accounts no longer has sell allowed, rounding unit, buyable products or left-out products, and the "Balance inside the account" heading is gone (#151).
- The Holdings and Rebalance panels no longer start with a heading, and the Holdings sub-tab is now called List (#148). A saved sub-tab choice keeps working.
- Schema version 17 removes the rebalancing account (`settings.target_account`, edit `SetTargetAccount`, the select in Rebalance and Settings); an older file loses the setting when read, and the export's `settings.csv` no longer has the two rebalancing-account rows (#148).
- Products inside a category are brought toward equal weights taking what is held into account (#142): the default "Free" targeting no longer splits a purchase evenly (or sells in proportion to the account holding); it behaves like "Equal", so the product furthest below its share is raised first. A category still goes one way only, and a product that cannot be traded (excluded, not tradable, nothing to sell) is left out so the rest take the amount.

### Fixed

- Suggestion rounding keeps the total (#141): the amount that can be sold is now counted per product in whole rounding units (each holding rounded down to the unit), so a fixed sell and the sell side of a full rebalance no longer fail or mismatch when holdings are not multiples of the unit; a full rebalance lowers the trade to what whole units can deliver and reports the rest. Buys and sells still add up exactly to the applied amount. No saved format changes.

### Changed

- Rebalance sub-tab layout (#139, #140): every tab (All and each account) now has the suggestion form and result on top and the account's holdings list ("Balance inside the account") below. "All" shows the holdings of the rebalancing account; account tabs offer all four modes (full rebalance and sell a fixed amount were All-only) for the tab's account, still with `held_only`. Full rebalance no longer requires the rebalancing account: it trades inside the requested account (selling follows that account's `sellable` and the record types), so the `NoTargetAccount` / `NotTargetAccount` errors are gone. UI only plus that relaxation; no saved format changes.
- Holdings tab sub-tabs are now Holdings / Rebalance. The old Suggestions sub-tab is the first account tab of Rebalance, "All" (the rebalancing account, mode, amount and result work as before), followed by one tab per account (the old By account content). In an account tab a product the account does not hold (record value 0 or no record, any type) is out of scope: it is left out of the balance table and is never bought by the account's suggestion (`Request::held_only`, default false; `account_balance` lists only held products). "All" does not hide anything, but its suggestion also uses `held_only` for the rebalancing account, so it never starts buying a product that account does not hold (to start one, add a record for it first). A saved `suggestions` or `accounts` sub-tab choice opens Rebalance. No saved format changes.
- Coincheck prices come from the public sales (hanbaijo) rate `GET /api/rate/<code>_jpy` (`rate`, a decimal string) instead of the exchange board's ticker (#125), because that is the price the person trades at and the board lacks some currencies (e.g. MATIC). A currency is unpriced only when Coincheck answers `Invalid pair`. No saved format changes.
- The Groups, Categories and Accounts lists in the settings tab are tighter (#120): columns are sized to their content and kept close together instead of spanning the card, and each add form is now the last row of the same table, with its fields under the matching columns (the drag-handle column stays empty) and the "Add" button in the delete-button column. Layout only; no behavior or schema change.
- Delete buttons (all uses of the shared delete button) show their text in the error color, in both light and dark themes.
- The account type moves from the account to the record (#99, stage 1). An account is now a container with no type; a record (account, product, type) carries one of the built-in types (general, specific, NISA, old NISA, deposit, crypto assets; fixed in the app, names translated) or a custom type the user adds (`custom_types`, `AddAccountType`, `UpdateAccountType`, `RemoveAccountType`). One account can hold the same product under several types. `Account::kind` and `AccountKind` are gone; `SetHolding`, `RemoveHolding`, `SetHoldingWeight` and `HoldingInput` take an optional `account_type`, `SetRecordType` changes the type of a record (refused when the target exists), and an import without a type keeps the type of the only existing record of the product. Selling: NISA and old NISA records (and custom types marked not sellable) are never sold by a suggestion; the account's own `sellable` still applies. Points are no longer a type. Schema version 14 (an older app refuses the file); an older file is converted on load (`migrate.rs`): taxable -> specific, NISA/deposit/crypto -> the same, iDeCo and Points -> custom types made only if used, other -> not set. The Accounts card has no type column; a new "Account types" card manages custom types; the Records card has a type per record; the Holdings tab has a separate Type column (right of Account, or of Product when grouped by account; empty when the record has no type); `holdings.csv` has `account_type` instead of `account_kind` and `accounts.csv` has no `kind`. The built-in types also include NISA growth (`nisa_growth`) and NISA tsumitate (`nisa_tsumitate`), shown in the order general, specific, NISA, NISA growth, NISA tsumitate, old NISA, cash, crypto assets; all NISA types are never sold by a suggestion. "NISA" (`nisa`) stays and means a NISA whose frame is not decided (a migrated NISA record keeps it; split it by hand in the Records card). The display name of `deposit` is now Cash (Japanese 現金); the id and the data are unchanged. Reading the type from table titles and filtering by it are stage 2.

### Added

- The window size, position and maximized state are remembered across restarts (#138), using the first-party `tauri-plugin-window-state` plugin (Apache-2.0 OR MIT). The state is saved when the window closes in `.window-state.json` in the app data folder (no portfolio data); a saved position that is off every connected screen is ignored and the window opens at the default position. No capability or CSP change (the frontend does not call the plugin); no saved format changes to the portfolio file.
- Binance balances through a read-only API key (#128): a new `binance` crate (same shape as `gmocoin`) reads the signed `GET /api/v3/account` (HMAC-SHA256 over the query string, `X-MBX-APIKEY` header) and the public `GET /api/v3/ticker/price`, against the fixed `https://api.binance.com` only. Holdings are valued with the last price of the asset's JPY market, otherwise its USDT market times `USDTJPY`; an asset with neither is reported as unpriced. The price basis (`board` / `board_via_usdt`) is kept per holding and named on screen. The key lives only in the OS keychain (`binance-api-key` / `binance-api-secret`); a new Binance card reuses the shared import plan view and remembers the last chosen account. Unconfirmed assumption: the target is Binance Japan, whose API Binance does not document; the host is fixed to the global API on the user's assumption that a Binance Japan key works there, and a rejected key shows Binance's own error text. To check on the first real fetch: the key is accepted, the permission names on the key screen, and whether an IP restriction is required.
- The exchange cards (bitFlyer, Coincheck, GMO Coin, Binance) remember the last chosen "Account to update" per exchange and use it as the default next time, also after a restart. It is a display preference kept in the webview's local storage (key `exchange-account:<exchange>`, shared helper `src/exchange-account.ts`), not in the portfolio data file; a remembered account that no longer exists is ignored and the crypto-accounts-first default applies. Fetch recipes are unchanged (their account is saved per recipe). No saved format changes.
- GMO Coin balances through the read-only API (#126): a new `gmocoin` crate (same shape as `coincheck`: `Credentials` with a hidden `Debug`, a `Transport` trait, signing, `Client`) reads `GET /private/v1/account/assets` with `API-KEY` / `API-TIMESTAMP` / `API-SIGN` (HMAC-SHA256 of timestamp + method + path + body), only with `GET` against the fixed `https://api.coin.z.com` and no redirects. Values use the response's `conversionRate`, which the API documents as the sales desk (hanbaijo) selling price, so no second request is needed; the four exchange-only assets (FCR, ASTR, NAC, WILD) use the exchange's last price instead and are marked `board`, and the card names them. An asset without a rate is reported as unpriced. Keys live only in the OS keychain (`save_gmocoin_credentials`, `has_gmocoin_credentials`, `delete_gmocoin_credentials`, `fetch_gmocoin_holdings`), and the import link step is the shared one. The shared API pieces are not extracted into a common crate (see the Extraction Policy decision in `docs/architecture.md`). Not verified against the real service yet.
- The import link step helps with existing products (#131), for every importer that shares it: a name equal to a product's name (trimmed, ignoring case) opens with that product selected, a name with a similar product (equal ignoring spaces and symbols, or one contains the other, at least two characters) gets a highlighted row and an info icon naming the product, and that product is offered first; nothing is linked until the person clicks. The rule is computed in Rust (`ImportPlan::suggestions`, only among products that could be linked). A refused link or create now shows its reason next to the row in the error color; a name conflict reads "A product with this name already exists. Link to it instead." and selects that product. The info icon is now shared (`infoIcon` in `dom.ts`). No saved format changes.
- Coincheck balances through the read-only API (#125): a new `coincheck` crate (same shape as `bitflyer`: `Credentials` with a hidden `Debug`, a `Transport` trait, signing, `Client`) reads `GET /api/accounts/balance` with `ACCESS-KEY` / `ACCESS-NONCE` / `ACCESS-SIGNATURE` (HMAC-SHA256 of nonce + URL; the nonce is the time in milliseconds and always increases), only with `GET` against the fixed `https://coincheck.com`, no redirects. The amount held is the free amount plus the amount reserved by open orders (lending, debt and tsumitate are not counted). Prices come from the public ticker (`last`) per held currency and are recorded with the quantity; a currency with no JPY pair is reported as unpriced and left out. The key and secret live only in the OS keychain (`coincheck-api-key`, `coincheck-api-secret`). New commands `save_coincheck_credentials`, `has_coincheck_credentials`, `delete_coincheck_credentials`, `fetch_coincheck_holdings` and a Coincheck card in the import tab (same steps as bitFlyer). Not verified against the real service yet. No saved format changes.

- The fetch recipes card is simpler (#117): a recipe has one name (the preset's label, translated), shown in the list and in the picker; rows are short (name, built-in badge, account select, "Fetch this recipe", delete) and the route description moved into an info-icon tooltip that shows on hover and keyboard focus; "Make my recipe" (detach) is gone from the screen (`DetachPresetRecipe` stays in the backend). The account of a recipe can be changed in its row (`Edit::SetRecipeAccount`, validated in Rust and undoable; a preset recipe cannot move to an account that already has that preset). The always-visible "Add an account" is replaced by a "New account..." entry in the add form's account select, which shows a name field only then. Recipes are grouped under a heading per site (the company name a preset gives, else the host), each with "Fetch this site"; "Fetch all" sits right below the card description (the three fetch buttons share one column and start at the same x) and the scope select is gone. The "Open the login pages" button is gone: a fetch whose site has no open tab starts the browser if needed, opens that site's login page (the app only navigates; nothing is typed or clicked) and asks the person to log in and press Fetch again, while the other sites go on. Every shipped site now has a login page (PayPay Securities: its top page; the person opens the holdings page by hand, which the message says), so the old "a page of the site must be open" error no longer shows for a missing tab. Those sites are held back even when the browser restored a login tab on start, and a "link not found" failure gets a "you may not be logged in" hint. `open_login_pages` takes the sites to open; `list_presets` also returns `site` and `company`. The add form hides presets already added as recipes (any account), starts on "---" for both the preset and the account, and keeps "Add" disabled until both are chosen (Rust still refuses a duplicate preset on one account). The add form has a "Add from a preset" heading and uses the same grid as the rows (preset in the name column, account next, "Add" in the delete column). The card no longer has "Open browser", "Close browser" or "Refresh pages": a "Browser settings" heading keeps only the browser and profile folder (the fetch starts the browser itself and the app closes it on exit; Open/Close stay in the "Fetch in browser" card), and the page select is gone: a recipe now must name the site it reads (`Edit::SetRecipe` refuses an empty `start_site`; the "Save as a recipe" form has a required site field; a recipe of a preset takes its site from the preset). A recipe stored earlier without a site still loads (no schema change), is listed under "No site" with Fetch disabled and a hint to delete it and save it again, and is never deleted automatically. No schema change.

- Account type on import and in the holdings table (#99, stage 2): a recipe can carry `type_rules` (a table title prefix -> a built-in or custom type id), written by the person or shipped by a preset, never guessed; with `all_tables`, each table of the page is read on its own and its records get the type its title stands for (the longest matching rule wins; a table that fits no rule is read without a type). The SBI domestic stocks and investment trusts presets map the six custody titles (specific, general, NISA, old NISA, NISA growth, NISA tsumitate) to types; the titles still have to be checked on the real page. A fetch now replaces per account and type: when every recipe of an account has rules, only the records of the types the rules name (and the records without a type) are replaced and the account's other records stay; one recipe without rules makes the whole account the scope as before (`Edit::ReplaceHoldings` takes an optional `types`; an input outside them is refused and no type is inherited from the existing record). Import plans keep rows of one product but different types apart and show a Type column when some row has one. The holdings table can be grouped by type (subtotals from `Overview::types`) and filtered to one type (subtotals are left empty while filtered). Removing a custom type removes the rules naming it. No schema change (`type_rules` is optional).

- Cross-check the page's evaluation against quantity x price (#100): a mapping can read the evaluation column together with the quantity and the price (`evaluation_column`, `evaluation_part`; needs the price column), a kind rule that reads the evaluation now also records the price when the mapping has a price column. The evaluation stays the value; quantity x price / price unit is only a check (`ImportRow::computed`). `ImportPlan::differences` lists the rows that differ by more than one smallest unit of the currency (the recipe's, else the linked product's, else 1), and the confirmation screen shows them (name, read value, computed value) without stopping the import. Rows without a quantity or a price are not checked. The Daiwa Connect domestic stocks preset now also keeps the current price (value column is the quantity, price column 5, evaluation column 6) and the Rakuten stocks rows keep their price. Schema version 15 (an older app refuses the file); no conversion is needed. The SBI evaluation column is not added yet (its position needs a check against the real page).

- Set or change the category of many products at once (#113): `Edit::SetProductsCategory { products, category | new_category }` validates everything first (empty list, unknown product or category, both or neither of category and new name, and the usual category name rules are refused) and then sets the category of all products, optionally creating the category in the same edit (target 0, no group), so it is one save and one undo step. The Records card has a checkbox per product and a bar with select all shown, select none, select the products of a category, and apply (existing or new category). No schema change.
- bitFlyer prices from the public price page (#103): the balance fetch can value every currency, including those with no yen market on Lightning, with the price from the table of `https://bitflyer.com/ja-jp/virtual-currency-chart` (inside the bitFlyer card: "Open the price page" starts the dedicated browser at the fixed address, and "Fetch balances" reads the table of that tab read-only and finds it by its headings, so no other card is needed; the app does not connect to the page). One price basis for all currencies; quantity and price are recorded. The Lightning last price is read too and shown next to the page price as a reference only (a difference never stops the import; a failing comparison price is ignored). A currency the page does not list is reported and left out, not valued with the API price. New commands `fetch_bitflyer_holdings_with_page_prices` and `open_bitflyer_price_page`, `bitflyer::PagePrices`; no saved format changes (nothing new is saved).

- Reorder accounts, groups, and categories by drag and drop (#111): `Edit::MoveAccountTo`, `MoveGroupTo` and `MoveCategoryTo` move a row to a given place (`index` is the place afterwards; out-of-range index and unknown id are refused, the same place is a no-op, the other rows keep their order). The three lists share one frontend part (`src/sortable.ts`, pointer events on a grip, no dependency): the dragged row is dimmed, a line shows the drop position, Esc cancels, and the moved row is highlighted for a moment (also after the up/down buttons, which stay). The order computation is in `src/reorder.ts` with tests.
- Groups and categories can be reordered with up/down buttons like accounts (#106): `Edit::MoveGroup` and `Edit::MoveCategory` swap with the neighbor (no-op at the ends, unknown id refused). The stored array order is the order, so the chart colors and legend follow it and no schema change is needed. The settings tab now shows the cards in the order groups, categories, accounts, all records.
- The fetch recipes screen has the same browser part as "Fetch in browser" (browser choice, profile folder, Open/Close browser) below "Open the login pages" (#98); it is one shared component (`views/browser-controls.ts`) using the same Tauri commands. The last browser kind (Chrome, Edge, or Brave) and profile folder are kept in a local file (`browser-settings.json` in the app data folder, command `get_browser_settings`; saved when the browser started) and preselected in both places after a restart; a remembered browser that is no longer found falls back to the first one found. They are not stored in the portfolio data file, and the path rules are unchanged. The profile folder was kept in the webview's local storage before; that is replaced.
- Card reading, stage 1 (#76): a recipe can read repeated blocks ("cards") under a heading instead of a table (`Recipe::cards` = `CardSource { heading, item_depth }`, exclusive with the table title; presets can describe it as data). A fixed read-only script (`crates/browser/js/read_cards.js`, isolated world) finds exactly one visible heading that starts with the text, takes the blocks `item_depth` levels below the region after it, and returns one row per card with each text line as a cell, so the existing import mapping, aliases and replace flow are reused. A page cut at a limit (50 headings, 500 items, 20 lines, 1,000 characters) or with no card is refused; no class names, attributes or link targets are used and nothing is clicked. - Card reading, stage 2 (#76): "Fetch with the browser" lists the card lists of the page next to its tables (one candidate per heading, at the first item depth that gives cards; label "N cards × M lines: heading"); choosing one loads one row per card into the import, and "Save as recipe" stores `cards` (heading and item depth) instead of a table title. Running a cards recipe waits (up to 10 s) for the heading and cards and for two equal reads, then goes through the same extract, alias and replace flow as a table (`ReadSource` in `browser_session.rs`).
- PayPay Securities preset (#76, stage 3), `paypay-sec-holdings` (provisional): reads the cards under 保有銘柄 on the page the person opens by hand after logging in (the mini app entry needs a click, which the app does not make; evaluation only: the page shows no quantity or price). - Merge two products and split an alias out (#101): `Edit::MergeProducts` moves the aliases and the records of one product to another of the same currency (different currencies are refused; the category of the kept product wins; where an account holds both, `on_conflict` picks the record that stays) and removes the first; `Edit::SplitProduct` cuts an alias out as a new product with the records of the accounts the person picks (records have no source name). A split is undone by merging the new product back. In the Records card, each product has a "Merge or split" fold. No schema change.

The name box grows with the name (and shows it whole as a tooltip), and the merge choices are no longer clipped to 11rem by the table-cell rule for selects; the cause was CSS (a fixed width and `td select` max-width), not a shortening of the text.

### Changed

- Holdings tab columns are now price (with the product's currency), quantity, value (the stored value, in the product's currency), value (JPY), and share.
- The rows left out of a fetch are listed with their reasons in the fetch result ("row 2 (not a number)"), as they were in the table import, instead of only a count.
